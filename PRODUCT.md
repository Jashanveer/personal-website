# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, founders, and potential collaborators evaluating Jashanveer Singh's product judgment and full-stack engineering work. They need to understand what he builds, how deeply he contributes, and where to continue the conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Jashanveer's work as shipped, credible software rather than a list of technologies. Success means a visitor can quickly recognize his range across native apps, backend systems, and web products, open the work that interests them, and reach out with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Technical, assured, and tactile. The portfolio should feel precise without becoming clinical, expressive without becoming theatrical, and personal without relying on novelty copy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer-portfolio templates, fake product previews, noisy technology dumps, decorative motion, and visual treatments that compete with the actual work. Do not turn the site into a dashboard or an agency-style concept piece.

## Design Principles

- Lead with real products and direct evidence.
- Let interaction communicate state and hierarchy.
- Keep the path from project to live product immediate.
- Preserve a consistent personal voice across technical disciplines.
- Make ambitious motion optional through reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic links, keyboard-visible focus, readable contrast in light and dark themes, responsive layouts, and a complete static experience for people who prefer reduced motion.
